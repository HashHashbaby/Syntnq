Match the sound to its picture

You will hear 12 short clips of music (20 seconds each). For each clip, four pictures are shown. Each picture is a "signature" generated automatically from a song's sound. Choose the picture you think belongs to the clip you heard. There are no right or wrong feelings here, just your best guess. It takes about 10 minutes. Headphones are best.

About this study

This is a small research test of whether people can recognise a song's visual signature. We record only your choices, how long each choice took, and the two optional answers below. No name, email or audio recording is collected, and nothing is sent anywhere: at the end you save a small results file and send it to the person who gave you this page. Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time by closing this page (nothing is kept).

Press play. You can replay the clip as often as you like.

Thank you
